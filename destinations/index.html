The many faces of Bhutan

Bhutan changes as you travel.

The landscape changes.

The architecture changes.

The climate changes.

And so does the rhythm of life.

Rather than rushing through every destination, we help you discover the places that best fit your journey.

01 · Where your Bhutan journey begins

Paro

Surrounded by mountains and steeped in history, Paro is where many journeys into Bhutan begin.

Discover ancient temples, traditional architecture, beautiful landscapes and the iconic journey to Taktsang.

But leave time to experience Paro beyond its landmarks.

Experience Paro
02 · The Kingdom today

Thimphu

Bhutan’s capital offers an intriguing meeting of tradition and modern life.

Discover local markets, traditional arts and crafts, spiritual sites, architecture, cuisine and the everyday rhythms of Bhutanese life.

Experience Thimphu
03 · A valley of warmth and beauty

Punakha

With its lush landscape, rivers, villages and magnificent dzong, Punakha offers a different atmosphere from the high mountain valleys.

Slow down here. Explore. Walk. Look beyond the obvious.

Experience Punakha
04 · A place to breathe

Phobjikha

Wide open landscapes and quiet mountain surroundings make Phobjikha a place for travelers who appreciate space and stillness.

The valley is also known for the black-necked cranes that visit during the winter months.

Experience Phobjikha
05 · Into Bhutan’s spiritual heartland

Bumthang

Ancient temples, sacred sites, traditional communities and beautiful valleys make Bumthang one of Bhutan’s most rewarding destinations for travelers seeking depth and discovery.

Experience Bumthang
06 · The quieter Bhutan

Haa

For travelers who want to venture beyond the familiar, Haa offers a more secluded experience of the Kingdom.

Mountain landscapes, traditional villages, forests and a slower pace create an intimate sense of place.

Experience Haa
07 · Where the mountains tell stories

Trongsa

Dramatically positioned among Bhutan’s central mountains, Trongsa offers history, architecture and spectacular scenery.

Its location makes the journey itself part of the experience.

Experience Trongsa
08 · For the curious traveler

Eastern Bhutan

Further from the traditional routes lies another Bhutan. Less visited. More remote. Rich in culture and extraordinary landscapes.

For travelers with the time and curiosity to venture farther, eastern Bhutan offers an entirely different perspective.

Discover Eastern Bhutan
