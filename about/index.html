Who we are

Bhutan is not simply somewhere to visit. It is a country to experience.

Its mountains, monasteries, villages, traditions, spirituality and people each reveal a different part of the Kingdom.

At NorLha Tours and Travels, we create private journeys for travelers who want to experience Bhutan personally, thoughtfully and at their own pace.

Our approach begins with a simple idea: listen first. Then create.

The NorLha approach

Listen first. Then create.

Four simple commitments sit behind every NorLha journey — from the first conversation to the moment you leave Bhutan.

We listen

Every traveler arrives with a different idea of Bhutan. Some come for culture. Some for the mountains. Some for spirituality. Some for adventure. Others simply want to slow down. We begin by understanding what Bhutan means to you.

We curate

We then shape an itinerary around your interests, pace, travel style and expectations. The journey can be refined until it feels right.

We connect

Bhutan is best experienced through its people. Our journeys create opportunities to discover local culture, traditions, stories, food, crafts and everyday life.

We coordinate

Behind every effortless journey is careful planning. We coordinate accommodation, transportation, guiding, activities and the many details that make your time in Bhutan comfortable and seamless.

Travel with freedom

We believe private travel should give you choice.

Choice of pace

Choice of experiences

Choice of accommodation

Choice of how you spend your time

Rather than asking you to fit into a predetermined journey, we create the journey around you.

Where you stay

Comfort without compromising character.

From intimate boutique properties to distinguished luxury hotels and retreats, we can arrange accommodation according to your preferences, itinerary and availability. The same applies to transportation and guiding.

Your journey should have the right balance of comfort, privacy, authenticity and flexibility.
